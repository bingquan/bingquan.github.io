Key takeaways

The summit converges on a practical thesis: successful agent improvement needs a strong harness around the model, not just a stronger model.

01 · Evaluate trajectories

Local success does not compose

Long, dependent tasks degrade sharply. Measure recovery, chain length, and retry behavior—not only final one-shot reward.

02 · Bound the loop

Keep success criteria outside the improver

Verifiers, promotion rules, and sealed telemetry are part of the safety mechanism, not tools the candidate should rewrite.

03 · Treat data as capability

Evidence access requires governance

Retrieval quality, provenance, and least privilege shape what an agent can safely learn from operational traces.

04 · Optimize operations too

Reward is necessary, not sufficient

Cost, latency, infrastructure errors, and repeatability determine whether an apparent improvement is deployable.
